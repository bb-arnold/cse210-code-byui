This program allows you to play a game of microchess. It adheres almost entirely to the rules of chess including almost all normal piece movement restrictions, pawn promotion, and winning conditions. The only exceptions are; the Knight isn't the only piece it allows to jump over other pieces, it doesn't require you to get your King out of check immediately, and - as is an extra rule in microchess - pawns can't move 2 spaces at the start (and thus there is no En Passent moves allowed either). Also, pawns promote automatically into a Queen upon reaching the end (that's not really a problem). It uses the chess coordinate system with a small adjuestment to move pieces. Rather than entering something like Ka4 to move your king to a4, you enter the coordinate of the space you want to move the piece from then where you want to move the piece to (you also use capital letters rather than lowercase). After one player takes the other's king, a message is printed which congratulates the winning player on his/her achievement. I decided not to clear the console after every move because it can be fun sometimes to look at how the game went afterward to see if there was anything you could have done differently. To print the chess board, I used a table object from the Spectre.Console .NET library. I used this documentation webpage to learn how to use one https://spectreconsole.net/console/widgets/table/.
